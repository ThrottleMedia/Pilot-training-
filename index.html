Know Your Aviation Authority

The South African Civil Aviation Authority (SACAA) is the national civil aviation authority. This section is designed to help you understand where official information comes from.

SACAA Licensing

Use the official SACAA Personnel Licensing information for current licensing requirements, forms, notices and related information.

Open SACAA Licensing ↗

SACAA Examinations

Check the current SACAA examination information and official study-material references.

Open SACAA Exams ↗

Aeronautical Information

The AIP contains official aeronautical information. Always use the current official publication when information is operationally relevant.

Open SACAA AIP ↗

Approved Training Organisations

SACAA provides an ATO verification portal. Confirm training approval before relying on a training provider.
